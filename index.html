Every Metra train, right now

This is a live map of the whole Metra commuter rail system around Chicago. Each arrow is a real train, placed from its own GPS report and updated about every 30 seconds. The fading trail shows where it just was.

I built it for fun. I wanted something calm to leave running on my second monitor, like an aquarium, except it's Chicago's trains moving around instead of fish.

  • MoveDrag to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. The ⛶ button (or 0) brings the whole network back.
  • TrainsClick any train to see where it is and where it's heading.
  • StationsClick a station, then ☆, to pin it to your board (up to 5) with Metra's live arrival times.
  • LinesPick a line at the bottom to focus on just that one.
  • AlertsThe ⚠ button at the top shows Metra's service alerts.
  • MoodSound and light/dark sit next to the title. Keys: M sound, T theme.
You can open this again any time with the ? next to the title.
